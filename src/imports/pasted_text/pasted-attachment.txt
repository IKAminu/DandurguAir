Design a complete, production-ready website UI/UX for **Dandurgu Air Travel & Tours Ltd**, a Nigerian travel company established in 2008, offering **Umrah, Hajj, flights, and tours & travel**.

The website's primary goals are:

1. Build immediate trust.
2. Make Dandurgu feel established, competent and easy to deal with.
3. Turn visitors into qualified travel enquiries.
4. Make applying for Umrah extremely easy.
5. Reduce friction in the customer application process.
6. Present Dandurgu as a modern, professional travel company without losing the warmth and human assistance of a traditional travel agency.

Do NOT make the design feel like a generic Nigerian travel agency website, a cheap airline website, a luxury hotel website, or an overly decorative Islamic website.

The overall visual direction is:

**TRUSTWORTHY + CALM + EASY + ESTABLISHED + MODERN**

Use Dandurgu's existing brand colours as the foundation:

* Primary: Dandurgu's distinctive purplish blue
* Secondary/accent: Dandurgu orange
* Supporting: white, off-white/light neutral backgrounds, dark text and very subtle neutral tones

If the exact colour values are not provided, create clearly labelled colour placeholders such as:
`PRIMARY BLUE — REPLACE WITH DANDURGU BRAND BLUE`
`ACCENT ORANGE — REPLACE WITH DANDURGU BRAND ORANGE`

Do not invent a completely different colour palette.

Use a modern, highly readable sans-serif typeface. Typography should feel trustworthy and professional rather than futuristic or overly luxurious.

Use generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, rounded but restrained cards/buttons, clean grids and excellent mobile responsiveness.

Avoid:

* Excessive gradients
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Huge decorative illustrations
* Stock-photo collages
* Excessive Islamic patterns
* Excessive orange
* Excessive blue
* Crowded sections
* Unnecessary animations represented in the UI
* Generic airplane icons everywhere
* Fake-looking certification badges
* Overly corporate SaaS aesthetics

The design should feel like a company that has been operating successfully for years and has finally built a modern digital front door.

# 12. START YOUR JOURNEY APPLICATION

This is the most important interactive experience on the website.

Do NOT design it as a traditional long form.

Design it as a **progressive, one-question-at-a-time application flow**.

The psychological principle is:

**EASY QUESTIONS FIRST → QUALIFICATION → MORE PERSONAL INFORMATION → DOCUMENTATION**

The user should never initially see a huge form containing name, email, phone and passport information.

The form should feel like a guided conversation.

# MOBILE DESIGN

Design mobile layouts for every major page.

The website must be designed mobile-first because a significant portion of Dandurgu's traffic will come from Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

For the application form specifically:

* One question at a time
* Large touch targets
* Very clear selected states
* Minimal typing wherever possible
* Dropdowns/selectors instead of unnecessary free text
* Sticky or clearly visible Next/Continue control where appropriate
* Back button always available
* Progress indicator remains visible
* Do not make the user scroll through unrelated questions
* Preserve entered information when navigating backwards

---

# FORM UX PRINCIPLES

The form should follow this exact psychological progression:

**LOW FRICTION**
Simple yes/no questions.

↓

**QUALIFICATION**
Travel timing, payment preference, group size and service.

↓

**COMMITMENT**
Travel details.

↓

**PERSONAL INFORMATION**
Name, date of birth, contact details.

↓

**DOCUMENTATION**
Passport details and upload.

↓

**SPECIAL REQUIREMENTS**
Assistance and guarantor information where relevant.

↓

**SUBMISSION**
Review and submit.

The user should feel that the form is becoming more detailed because they are progressing toward an actual travel application, not because Dandurgu is collecting information for its own sake.

# FINAL DESIGN REQUIREMENT

Create the website as a coherent reusable design system, not isolated mockups.

Prioritize:
**Trust**
**Ease**
**Clarity**
**Conversion**
**Human assistance**

The website should communicate:

**“Dandurgu has been doing this for years. They've built a modern way for me to get started.”**

The final design should be sophisticated enough to represent an established travel company, but simple enough that someone arriving from an Instagram or Facebook ad can understand what Dandurgu does and begin an application within seconds.

The most important user journey to optimize is:

**Meta Ad → Umrah Page → START YOUR JOURNEY → Qualification → Application → WhatsApp / Dandurgu follow-up**
